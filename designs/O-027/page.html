What it does

One score per session

Separate layers feed one fused score, so no single trick decides.

Shows why

Each result lists the layers that fired and what each layer alone would have done.

Keeps a dated ledger

Every drill that moves a number is logged. A4 and A5 went from missed on 1 October to challenged on 4 October.

How it works

  1. Read in layers

    Network, script, automation, behavior and agent layers are each checked alone.

  2. Fuse into one score

    The layers are combined. One layer alone can raise a challenge, never a block.

  3. Act by level

    The result is allow, slow, challenge or block.

Proof

Live drill of attacker classes A0 to A5 and 5 scripted humans on our own sites: every attacker class was challenged, every human was allowed.
